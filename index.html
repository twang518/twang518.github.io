Projects

DM-Seq

Direct Methylation Sequencing (DM-Seq) is a fully enzymatic sequencing method that detects 5-methylcytosine (5mC) at single-base resolution. This work represents the technological foundation of two patents and a pre-seed diagnostic company ACE Genomics Inc. Wang T, Fowler JM, Liu L, Loo CE, Luo M, Schutsky EK, Berríos KN, DeNizio JE, Dvorak A, Downey N, Montermoso S, Pingul B, Nasrallah M, Gosal W, Wu H, Kohli RM. Unnatural DNA modifications enable direct enzymatic sequencing of 5-methylcytosine at single base resolution. Nature Chemical Biology 2023. 19, 1004–1012.

5cxmC

I discovered the new DNA base 5-carboxymethylcytosine (5cxmC) for the first time. Unexpectedly, I found that only a single point mutation in an endogenous methyltransferase safeguards the E. coli genome from creating 5cxmC from the trace metabolite carboxy-SAM (CxSAM). Wang T and Kohli, RM. Discovery of an Unnatural DNA Modification Derived from a Natural Secondary Metabolite. Cell Chemical Biology. 2021, 28, 1-8.
